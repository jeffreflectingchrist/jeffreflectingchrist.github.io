Substack copy page: English

Type or paste your post, then copy it into Substack. Headings, bold, italics and images keep their formatting.

  1. Type or paste the title, then press Copy title and paste it into the Title field on Substack.
  2. Type or paste the post text into the Post box.
  3. For a Bible passage, press + Add Bible passage box, copy the verses from the Bible site, click the yellow box and paste. Footnote marks are removed.
  4. Press Copy post, then paste into the Substack body.

Substack 复制页：中文

输入或粘贴文章，再复制到 Substack。标题、粗体、斜体和图片的格式都会保留。

  1. 输入或粘贴标题，点复制标题，粘贴到 Substack 的标题栏。
  2. 把正文输入或粘贴到正文框。
  3. 需要引用经文时，点+ 添加经文框，到经文网站复制经文，再点黄色框粘贴。脚注和参照符号会自动去掉，节数保留为小号数字。
  4. 点复制正文，粘贴到 Substack 正文。
